# Use
As with Oracle, each invocation of Veilwalker's Sight requires a main action and that the user must Commit Effort for the day. Once triggered, the Veilwalker gets a vision related to the question about the future that they're asking. This vision must always be from the personal vantage point of the Veilwalker, and never more than a minute of insight. 

# Techniques
## Future Echoes
The Veilwalker gains a brief vision related to the question about the future that they're asking of. This vision is always from their own personal vantage point and never reveals more than a minute of insight, though the Veilwalker processes it almost instantly as part of the power's use

This ability works on a tiered system, the farther the Veilwalker looks ahead, the less precise the vision is.
- A year in the future could provide possible broad strokes, while a week ahead vision might show specific possible events

## Retrospection
The Veilwalker gains the ability to learn information about the history of an object or location through touching the object or being present in a location.

## Insightful Threads
The Veilwalker focuses on a person and gains subtle insights about it over time. They could receive small, involuntary visions or impressions to assist the Veilwalker in anticipating plans or sense nearby threats
